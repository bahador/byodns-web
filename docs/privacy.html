Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 April 2026

FractalDNS has a focus on user privacy. FractalDNS does not share user data with third parties under any circumstances. The site does not and will never show ads. All revenue comes from paid subscriptions, removing the need for any type of tracking.

The app runs on your own device, and all third-party services or libraries are open source. There are no servers. FractalDNS does not store your payment information. FractalDNS earns all of its revenue through user subscriptions, and has no business relationship with surveillance-based platforms.

Crash Reporting

FractalDNS collects crash reports (device model, OS version, stack traces) via Firebase Crashlytics. No personal information is collected. The advertising ID is disabled.

This data is processed by Google and may be stored on servers outside the EU. Crash data is retained for 2 months. Google Signals and all advertising features are disabled.

Your Rights

You can opt out of analytics, clear all local data, and request deletion of collected data at any time. If you're in the EU, you have full GDPR rights including access, erasure, and portability. Contact support@fractaldns.com to exercise them.
